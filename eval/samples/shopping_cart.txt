SRD: Online Shopping Cart and Checkout v1.0

1. Cart
1.1 A customer shall be able to add a product to the cart; the quantity must be between 1 and 10 per product.
1.2 The cart shall display the unit price, quantity, line total and grand total, updated immediately when quantity changes.
1.3 A customer shall be able to remove an item from the cart.
1.4 Products that are out of stock shall not be addable to the cart.

2. Discounts
2.1 The system shall accept one coupon code per order; invalid or expired coupons shall show an error message.
2.2 Orders above 2000 INR shall receive free shipping.

3. Checkout
3.1 The customer shall provide a delivery address with a 6-digit PIN code before payment.
3.2 The system shall support UPI, credit/debit card and cash on delivery; cash on delivery is not available for orders above 10000 INR.
3.3 After successful payment the system shall send an order confirmation email within 1 minute.
3.4 If payment fails the order shall not be created and the cart contents shall be preserved.

4. Non-functional
4.1 The checkout page shall load within 3 seconds on a 4G connection.
4.2 Card details shall be processed through a PCI-DSS compliant gateway and never stored by the application.
