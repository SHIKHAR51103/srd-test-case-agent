SRD: Digital Wallet - Money Transfer v1.0

1. Add money
1.1 A verified user shall be able to add money to the wallet from a linked bank account; the minimum amount is 100 INR and the maximum is 50000 INR per transaction.
1.2 Wallet balance shall be updated within 10 seconds of a successful top-up.

2. Transfer
2.1 A user shall be able to transfer money to another wallet using the receiver's registered mobile number.
2.2 A transfer shall be rejected if the amount exceeds the available wallet balance.
2.3 The daily transfer limit shall be 100000 INR for fully verified users and 10000 INR for users with minimum KYC.
2.4 The user shall confirm every transfer with a 6-digit transaction PIN; after 3 wrong PIN entries transfers shall be blocked for 24 hours.

3. History and notifications
3.1 The user shall be able to view the last 12 months of transactions and filter them by date and type.
3.2 Both sender and receiver shall receive an SMS notification for every successful transfer.
3.3 Failed transactions shall be refunded automatically within 24 hours.

4. Non-functional
4.1 All transactions shall be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
4.2 The system shall support 1000 concurrent transfers without degradation.
